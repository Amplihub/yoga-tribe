FAQ

I've never done Reformer. Is it for me? −

Yes. Classes are low impact and beginner friendly, and your 1:1 check helps us tailor it to you.

I have a stiff back or tight hips. Can I join? +

Yes. Pramod checks your mobility first and gives you options in every class. If you have an injury or health condition, check with your doctor first.

When can I start? +

Whenever you want. Buy now and start any time up to 17 December, or from 27 January when we reopen. Start by 28 February. Started in December? Your weeks pause over the break and pick up from 27 January.

How big are the classes? +

Six people max, so you get real attention and hands-on help.

What do I bring? +

Comfy clothes, grip socks for Reformer, your yoga mat and a water bottle.

What happens after 4 weeks? +

You can join as a member at your locked-in price. No pressure.
